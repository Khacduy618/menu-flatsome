Bespoke watch straps, cut from the cleanest part of the hide.

Handdn designs bespoke watchstraps from a wide range of leathers. This page shows only the new navigation: a quiet header, a drawer that cascades on desktop and drills down on mobile, and search as its own sheet. Every link points to the live handdn.com.

Motion

Drawer 560ms, panels 420ms on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1). Rows fade in with a 32ms stagger. All of it collapses to instant with reduced motion.

Spacing

Header 64px (56 on mobile). Panels clamp(320px, 27vw, 392px) wide with 40px side padding. Rows sit on a 9 to 12px rhythm.

Type

One geometric sans in three weights. Level 1 at 19px / 300, level 2 at 15.5px, group labels at 11px uppercase, tracked .14em.

Keyboard

Arrows move and open, Esc steps back one level, then closes. Focus is trapped in the drawer and returns to the Menu button.
